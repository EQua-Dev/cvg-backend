# CVG FC — Player Profiling Questionnaire (v2, short words)

**Length:** 6 common + 8 for your position group = **14 taps, about 3 minutes**.

**How it looks in the app:**
- One question per screen, with a small pitch picture showing the moment.
- 2–4 big buttons, then it moves to the next question by itself.
- Progress dots at the top, and you can stop and come back anytime.
- No typing at all.

**Intro screen (shown to players):**
> **How do you play?**
> 14 quick taps. No wrong answers.
> [ **Start** ]

---

## Scoring key (hidden from players)

**Game plans:** **POS** Possession · **CTR** Counter-attack · **PRS** High press · **BLK** Defensive · **DIR** Direct

| Group | Roles |
|---|---|
| **GK** | **SWK** Sweeper-keeper · **SHS** Shot-stopper · **DST** Distributor · **CMD** Commander |
| **DEF** | **BPD** Ball-playing defender · **STP** Stopper · **CVR** Covering defender · **AFB** Attacking full-back · **DFB** Defensive full-back · **IFB** Inverted full-back |
| **MID** | **DLP** Deep playmaker · **BWM** Ball-winner · **B2B** Box-to-box · **AP** Attacking playmaker · **CAR** Ball-carrier |
| **ATT** | **POA** Poacher · **TGT** Target man · **INF** Inside forward · **PRF** Pressing forward · **LNK** Link forward · **WNG** Winger · **RUN** Runner in behind |

**Results:**
- **Game plan fit (0–100):** points earned ÷ the most points possible from the questions shown × 100.
- **Main role:** the top-scoring role. A second role is shown if it scores within 80% of the main one.
- **Profile label:** top game plan + main role, e.g. *"Counter-attacking Inside Forward"*.

## Extras

- **Second position:** if a player also plays in another group, they're offered 8 more taps for it (optional).
- **Consistency check:** A1 and A5 ask the same thing two ways. If the answers clash, the profile is marked "low confidence" for the coach.
- **Peer check:** in the rating window, teammates answer one extra question per player: "Best role for [Name]?" (tap one). The coach sees it next to the player's own answers.
- **Finish screen:** shows the result straight away, e.g. *"You're a **Counter-attacking Inside Forward**"*, with the five game plan bars and a **Share** button.
